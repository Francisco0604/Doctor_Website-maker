# BrightSmile Dental Care - Brand Guide

## Brand Identity
BrightSmile Dental Care is a premium, luxury dental brand focused on delivering world-class aesthetic and restorative dentistry. The brand exudes confidence, cleanliness, and clinical excellence with a touch of warmth and luxury.

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Deep Teal/Blue):** `#0F3443` - Representing professionalism, trust, and depth.
- **Secondary (Soft Gold):** `#D4AF37` - Symbolizing luxury, quality, and excellence.
- **Accent (Champagne):** `#F7E7CE` - For soft backgrounds and highlights.
- **Base (Pure White):** `#FFFFFF` - Cleanliness and clarity.
- **Text (Slate Gray):** `#334155` - Readability and modern feel.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Serif font (e.g., Playfair Display or System Serif) for a classic, trustworthy, and premium feel.
- **Body:** Sans-serif (e.g., Inter or System Sans) for modern readability and clinical precision.

## Logo Concept
A minimalist, stylized "S" that subtly forms a smile or a tooth silhouette, rendered in soft gold against a deep teal background.

## Design Principles
- **Spacious Layouts:** Use generous white space to evoke a feeling of calm and luxury.
- **Smooth Animations:** Utilize `framer-motion` for subtle fades and transitions.
- **High-Quality Imagery:** Use professional photography showing happy patients and state-of-the-art facilities.

## Deployment Instructions (Vercel)
1. Push the code to a GitHub repository.
2. Connect the repository to Vercel.
3. Ensure the `Framework Preset` is set to `Next.js`.
4. Add any necessary environment variables (e.g., for Contact Form integration).
5. Deploy.
