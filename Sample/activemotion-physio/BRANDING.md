# BRANDING: ActiveMotion Physiotherapy

## Core Identity
**Name:** ActiveMotion Physiotherapy  
**Tagline:** Move Better. Recover Faster. Live Pain-Free.  
**Vibe:** Energetic, Modern, Clinical Excellence, Movement-focused.

## Visual Language
### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Energetic Orange (`#ea580c` / `orange-600`) - Represents vitality, movement, and recovery.
- **Secondary:** Slate Gray (`#1e293b` / `slate-800`) - Represents professionalism, stability, and trust.
- **Accent:** Soft Orange (`#fff7ed` / `orange-50`) - Used for backgrounds and highlighting components.
- **Neutral:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) and Slate White (`#f8fafc`).

### Typography
- **Headings:** Sans-serif (Geist/Inter), Bold/Extrabold for an athletic, strong feel.
- **Body:** Sans-serif, clean and legible for medical information.

### Logo Concept
A stylized abstract "M" or "A" that mimics the silhouette of a person in motion or a muscle fiber curve, integrated with a forward-moving arrow in orange.

## Sitemap
1. **Home:** High-impact hero, clinical highlights, CTA.
2. **Conditions:** Searchable directory of what we treat.
3. **Treatments:** Specialized rehab programs and recovery timelines.
4. **Testimonials:** Success stories with data points (e.g., "Back to running in 4 weeks").
5. **Blog:** Movement tips and injury prevention.
6. **Appointment:** Smart booking with pain assessment.
7. **Contact:** Maps, parking, and direct access.

## Wireframe Strategy
- **Navigation:** Sticky with clear "Book Assessment" CTA.
- **Hero:** Large typography, high-motion imagery/backgrounds.
- **Social Proof:** Integrated rating badges and patient count.
- **Utility:** Floating WhatsApp and Pain Calculator for engagement.

## Deployment Instructions (Vercel)
1. Push code to GitHub.
2. Connect repository to Vercel.
3. Framework: Next.js.
4. Environment Variables: None required for this prototype.
5. Build Command: `npm run build`.
