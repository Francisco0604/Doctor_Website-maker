# 📞 Cold Calling Scripts & Phone Guide

## Phase 1: Passing the Gatekeeper (Receptionist / Front Desk)

**Objective**: Get transferred directly to the Doctor or Practice Manager without sounding like a generic salesperson.

> **Caller**: "Hi, good morning! Is Dr. [Doctor's Last Name] available for a quick minute? I'm calling regarding the digital patient booking setup for [Clinic Name]."
>
> **Gatekeeper**: *"Is this regarding a sales call / medical representative?"*
>
> **Caller**: "No, this is about your online Google Maps booking links for [Clinic Name]. I noticed a quick fix needed on mobile devices for patients trying to book appointments. Could you pass me through to Dr. [Name] or the Practice Manager?"

---

## Phase 2: Doctor Opening Hook (30-Second Elevator Pitch)

> **Caller**: "Hello Dr. [Doctor's Name], hope you're having a good day. My name is [Your Name] from **HealUX Studios**. I'll be brief—we build high-converting websites exclusively for doctors and medical clinics in India.
>
> Right now, over 70% of patients in [City/Area] search for clinics on their phones, but most clinic websites don't convert those visits into actual appointments. We build fast, high-trust websites with **1-click WhatsApp booking** and **Local Google Map ranking**.
>
> I've actually sent a live 30-second interactive preview to your phone. Have you got a quick moment to take a look?"

---

## Phase 3: Handling Common Objections

### Objection 1: *"We already have a website."*
> **Response**:  
> "That's great, Doctor! Many clinics we work with already had websites, but they weren't getting direct patient calls or WhatsApp bookings from them. 
> 
> Our platforms are specifically engineered as **Patient Acquisition Systems**—they load in under 0.5 seconds and include interactive tools like pain assessment calculators that turn visitors into walk-in consultations. 
> 
> Can I send you a 1-minute WhatsApp link to compare your current site against our live demo?"

---

### Objection 2: *"We rely purely on word-of-mouth recommendations."*
> **Response**:  
> "Word of mouth is the absolute best source of patients, Doctor. But today, even when a friend recommends your clinic, the first thing the patient does is search your clinic name on Google. 
> 
> If your site doesn't immediately show high-trust clinical authority and a simple WhatsApp booking button, up to 40% of those referrals end up calling another clinic nearby. We make sure every referral converts."

---

### Objection 3: *"How much does it cost?"*
> **Response**:  
> "Our packages start at ₹19,999 for complete turnkey setups with WhatsApp integration, hosting, and local SEO. Most of our doctor clients recover the entire investment within 2-3 weeks from just 3 or 4 new patient consultations. 
> 
> Let me send you our live showcase link so you can calculate your exact return using our interactive ROI calculator."

---

## Phase 4: Closing the Call (Securing Next Step)

> **Caller**: "Doctor, I'll send you our live showcase link right now on WhatsApp (**https://healux-studioswebsite.vercel.app**). Try opening it on your mobile phone—you can test our live sample sites for dental, physio, and general clinics.
> 
> Let's reconnect for 5 minutes tomorrow afternoon once you've seen it. Would 3 PM or 5 PM work better for you?"
