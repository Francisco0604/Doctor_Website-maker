# 📋 Client Onboarding & Proposal Template

## Section 1: Discovery & Intake Questionnaire
*(Ask these 5 questions during your initial consultation with the doctor or clinic owner)*

1. **Practice Specialty & Core Services**: What are your top 3 most profitable or high-demand treatments/services?
2. **Target Patient Demographic**: Where are most of your patients coming from (local neighborhood, regional city, referrals)?
3. **Current Booking Process**: How do patients currently schedule appointments (phone calls, walk-ins, WhatsApp)?
4. **Design Preferences**: Do you have existing brand colors, logos, or clinic photos you would like to showcase?
5. **Launch Timeline**: When would you like the new website to be live for patients?
