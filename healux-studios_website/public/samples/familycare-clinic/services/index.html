Our Medical Services

From routine check-ups to chronic disease management, we offer a wide range of services to keep your family healthy.

General Consultation

Routine health check-ups and medical advice for common ailments. We focus on early detection and prevention.

  • Physical Exams
  • Fever & Infection Treatment
  • Medical Referrals

Diabetes Management

Comprehensive care plan for Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, including continuous glucose monitoring advice.

  • Blood Sugar Tracking
  • Insulin Management
  • Dietary Counseling

Hypertension Care

Expert management of high blood pressure to prevent long-term complications like heart disease.

  • BP Monitoring
  • Lifestyle Modification
  • Medication Review

Pediatric Care

Gentle and professional healthcare services for infants, children, and adolescents.

  • Growth Monitoring
  • Immunizations
  • Common Childhood Illnesses

Cardiology Screening

Initial cardiac assessments including ECG and risk factor management for heart health.

  • ECG / EKG
  • Cholesterol Screening
  • Heart Disease Prevention

Laboratory Services

On-site blood tests and diagnostic services for fast and accurate results.

  • Blood Panels
  • Urine Analysis
  • Pathology Coordination

Health Certificates

Medical certificates for work, school, or travel after a thorough clinical assessment.

  • Work Fitness
  • Travel Clearance
  • Sports Physicals

Preventive Screenings

Regular screenings for cancer, vitamin deficiencies, and other health risks.

  • Cancer Screenings
  • Vitamin Profile
  • Bone Density Check
